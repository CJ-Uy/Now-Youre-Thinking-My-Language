## Findings to carry into the report

- **The length penalty is inert.** On the calibration data every penalty in the grid from 0 to 1.0 selects the same candidates. With two attempts, accuracy moves in 0.5 steps, and ties are already broken by length. So "feedback" versus "shortest" differs only in accuracy ranking, not in a tuned length trade-off. Evidence: `artifacts/scores/opus55-calibration-a2-20261110/penalty.json`, and the 20 November log entry.
- **Stability check (instructor feedback 3).** The two-attempt pick equals the eight-attempt pick in 64.6% of pools, above the pre-registered 0.60 threshold, so training used 2 attempts. Without three mislabelled calibration questions the figure is 65.2%.
- **Multilingual material is not shorter** under the Qwen tokenizer. Selected training inputs average English 61.0 vs multilingual 59.9 tokens (shortest), and 63.1 vs 62.3 (feedback). Trained multilingual outputs are also not shorter.
- **GSM8K label noise.** Four wrong or doubtful gold answers turned up among the 200 training-pool questions used (2.0%). Three of them are not in the community `hkust-nlp/gsm8k-fix` list. See `docs/DATASET_NOTES.md`, which cites Vendrow et al. 2025 and Truong et al. 2025.
- **AI review is not a substitute for human review.** In round 1 the AI pre-review approved 2 of the 4 rewrites the bilingual human rejected. That is why the series is labelled AI-assisted.
